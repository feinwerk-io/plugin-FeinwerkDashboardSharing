__Who can share a dashboard?__

Every signed-in Matomo user can share their own dashboards. Click *Share this dashboard* above the widgets, then choose *All users who can see one of your websites* or enter the logins of specific users. Users with admin access also get a list of users to tick.

__Who sees a shared dashboard?__

Only users who can see at least one of your websites, whether you share it with all users or with a list of logins. Super users see every share. If you and a subscriber no longer have a website in common, the copy keeps its last version and is no longer synchronised.

__What do recipients get?__

Their own copy of the dashboard, marked with a people icon in the dashboard menu. The copy is read-only and is synchronised with your original every time it is opened.

__Do recipients see data of websites they cannot access?__

No. Only the widgets and their layout are shared. Every recipient sees the numbers with their own Matomo permissions, so only for websites they can access.

__What happens when I stop sharing or remove the original?__

Subscribers keep the last version they received, but it is no longer synchronised. After *Stop sharing* the copy becomes an ordinary dashboard of the subscriber. If you remove the original with Matomo's *Remove dashboard*, its shares end as well.

__Does it work with per-widget segments?__

Yes, together with FeinwerkDashboardSegment: subscribers then also get the segment and time range of each widget. FeinwerkDashboardSharing works on its own as well.

__Does the plugin change Matomo?__

No. It modifies neither Matomo core nor the bundled Dashboard plugin and keeps shares and subscriptions in tables of its own.
